# Verified English Example Reports

This directory contains curated English reports produced for the maintained edition of AI Value Investing Agents.

These examples are not translations of the historical archive. Each report is newly prepared from attributable public sources and is intended to demonstrate the repository's research controls, calculation discipline, and decision structure.

## Required metadata

Every verified example must state:

- **Status:** `Verified example`
- **Research cutoff:** the final date included in the evidence set
- **Primary reporting period:** the filing or earnings period being analyzed
- **Workflow demonstrated:** the canonical repository workflow represented by the report
- **Source hierarchy:** primary documents first, secondary context separately labeled
- **Calculation policy:** formulas and derived values must be reproducible
- **Audit status:** what was checked and what remains judgment rather than fact
- **Limitations:** stale-data, comparability, accounting, and market-price limitations
- **Disclaimer:** educational research, not personalized investment advice

## Verification standard

A report may be labeled `Verified example` only when:

1. every material historical figure is traceable to a listed source;
2. derived figures show their formula or input set;
3. market context is explicitly separated from company filings;
4. incompatible segment definitions or fiscal periods are not silently compared;
5. uncertainty, estimates, scenarios, and judgment are visibly labeled;
6. no audit failure remains unresolved.

`Verified example` does not mean the report is complete, current forever, or independently audited by an accounting firm. It means the cited evidence and calculations were checked against the stated cutoff and scope.

## Update policy

Reports are point-in-time artifacts. Do not silently replace old facts with new ones. A material update should create a newly dated file or append a clearly dated update section while preserving the original cutoff.

## Disclaimer

These reports are for educational and research purposes only. They are not investment, legal, accounting, or tax advice. Market prices, filings, management roles, product definitions, and competitive conditions can change after the stated cutoff.
